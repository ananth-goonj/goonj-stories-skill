# What you get, and how to read it

Everything is written next to the stories in a folder called `Story Review <date>` (or the folder you chose). Names of files start with the batch label.

| file | what it is | who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| `... - Analysis Report.html` | The report. Open it in any browser. It works offline and on a phone. | The MEL team, internal only |
| `... - Method and Data.xlsx` | The workbook: summary counts with live formulas, one row for each story, the 19 Key Questions with the exact words, quotes, findings, corrections log, writer questions, codebook. | The MEL team |
| `... - Story-by-Story Readings.md` | A reading of each story: what it tells you, what is missing, questions for its writer, readiness. | Programme team, writers' leads |
| `RUN_LOG.md` | What went in, what was done, what needs a person's look. | Whoever ran the review |
| `_working/` | Records, logs and scripts behind the files. Holds names and story texts. | Keep with the outputs; do not share |

## The report, section by section
- **In brief:** answers to the six questions, each pointing to the section with the evidence.
- **The batch at a glance, each story, the work sections:** who did the work, why, how Goonj came in and who decided, what people put in, what changed, voice and feelings, kit. In a Full review these carry the findings, each with its strength, story ids, caveats and how it was counted.
- **The 19 Key Questions:** for each question, how many stories answer clearly, in part, or not at all; and a story-by-story grid with no totals.
- **What the stories leave out:** gaps counted across the batch. A gap is a gap in the write-up.
- **Readiness for outside use:** the 12 tests and a band from 1 to 5 for each story. Readiness measures the write-up, not the work.
- **Sensitivity and consent:** the consent register. It shows tier, where the detail sits and whether a community label is named, but never the type of detail for a story.
- **For the next stories and questions for the writers:** what to ask field writers to include.
- **How this was checked:** the method in numbers, and the limits.

## Reading the bands
5 publishable as it is after a copy-edit; 4 usable after light editing plus one field call; 3 usable with edits and a follow-up call; 2 needs substantial re-reporting; 1 cannot be used outside in any form. A reviewer can override a band with a written reason; the log lists every override.

## Reading Key Question answers
Clear: answered with specifics. Partial: in general words or with a stock phrase. Not stated: nothing answers it. A cell in the grid is a fact about the write-up.
