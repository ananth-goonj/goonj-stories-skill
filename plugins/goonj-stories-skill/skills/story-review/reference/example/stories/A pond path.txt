The Pond Path of Meghnapur

Meghnapur is a village in Sarayan Block of Kalindi district. About 140 families live here. For many years the narrow path to the village pond turned into mud during the rains, and women carrying water slipped and fell.

In August 2026 a team from Goonj held a meeting in the school courtyard. Savitri Rao, a resident who is 52 years old, stood up and said, “We have asked the panchayat three times to repair this path, and nobody came.” The women then listed three problems: the path, the broken handpump and the school roof. After a long talk they decided that the path should come first, because every household uses the pond.

Between 10 and 13 August 2026, 36 villagers, 22 women and 14 men, raised and levelled the path, which is 180 feet long and 6 feet wide. The Goonj team stayed on the site on the first day and showed how to lay the stones in layers. Each family that worked received a family kit with a bag of rice, a blanket and utensils.

The village gave 40 bags of stone from the old school wall and Raju Sahu lent his bullock cart for four days. The village women's group said that they will look after the path and fill the low places every year after the rains.

Ten days later, heavy rain fell for two nights. Savitri Rao said, “This time the path did not wash away, and I carried water without fear.” The head teacher, Mr. Ghosh, said, “The children now reach school dry.”
